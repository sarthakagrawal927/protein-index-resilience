Compare protein foods with the evidence attached

Protein Index is a source-aware catalog of foods sold in India. It separates verified label evidence from discovery data and makes incomplete records visible.

What you can compare

Browse active products by identity, nutrition status, ingredients, protein density, and catalog completeness. Missing values are not zero, and unverified values are not label-confirmed.

How to interpret the catalog

Protein Index is not a complete market census or medical advice. Every product page links to an equivalent Markdown record and the public JSON API.
